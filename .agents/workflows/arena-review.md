# /arena-review

Follow the critique-and-polish skill.

1. If a subagent or a fresh chat is available, hand the review to it with only the brief, the idea sentence, the screenshots and the critique-and-polish skill. Otherwise mark the review "single-context".
2. Capture or open screenshots at 1440, 768 and 390 px. Check each file is valid.
3. Do the fourteen passes before reading any scanner output.
4. Score design, usability, creativity, content; apply the caps; record the round.
5. Run slop-check and render-check; add their findings.
6. Fix the top three problems, preferring token-level fixes, in the fix order of the skill.
7. Re-render, rescore. One more round at most.
8. Show the human: screenshots, both rounds of scores, what is left, at most one question. Apply their corrections first.
