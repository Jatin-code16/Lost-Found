# Arena Playbook: Human Creativity × AI, 2 hours

The event: participants get challenges and limited time to produce the best result, under the theme Human Creativity × AI. The exact format is not known in advance. This playbook covers the likely shapes and tells you what to do in each.

## 1. Before the clock (ask the organisers, 2 minutes)

- Which AI tools are provided or allowed? Can I use my own files, this kit, the internet, my own photos?
- One long challenge or several short ones? Website, text, image, video, idea pitch, or mixed?
- What is judged: the output, the prompts, the process, a live demo, audience votes? Is there a presentation?
- Submission format and deadline. Solo or team?

Similar prompt contests judge some mix of: clarity and structure of the prompt, creativity and originality, relevance of the output to the task, use of constraints, and ethical use. Some include an "AI or human?" spotting round. Assume all of these can be judged unless told otherwise.

If you cannot ask: assume output and process are both judged, only provided tools are allowed, and everything must work when first opened.

## 2. The principle

The AI is fast and average, and other contestants' AIs have the same average. You are slow and specific. Spend your minutes on the few decisions only you can make: the detail, the choice among options, the cut, the voice. Let the AI type. Make your authorship visible in the work and in the note.

## 3. Run of show: one website challenge (120 minutes)

| Time | What | Lead | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:10 | Pen before prompt (START-HERE.md). Three own ideas, one true detail, three must-nots, one action. | You | Notes on paper |
| 0:10 to 0:15 | `/arena-brief` with your notes. Answer its questions. | AI asks, you answer | Inventory and argument written |
| 0:15 to 0:27 | `/arena-diverge`. Ten ideas with probabilities, the deal, three directions. | AI proposes, you choose | You picked or mixed (CP2) |
| 0:27 to 0:40 | `/arena-build` steps 1 to 4: picks block, tokens, copy deck, first screen. Look at it. | AI, you steer | First screen right at desktop and phone. **Save copy 1** |
| 0:40 to 1:15 | Sections in argument order, the live thing, imagery. | AI, you steer | Main journey works end to end. **Save copy 2** |
| 1:15 to 1:30 | `/arena-review` round 1. Fresh-context reviewer if you can. Fix top 3. | AI, you correct (CP3) | Scores up, no FAILs |
| 1:30 to 1:42 | Human signature pass: headline, first line, button, one line only you could write. Remove one thing that says nothing. | You | It sounds like you. **Save copy 3** |
| 1:42 to 1:50 | Review round 2 if needed. Phone check. Keyboard check. | AI | No overflow, focus visible |
| 1:50 to 2:00 | `/arena-freeze`. Process note. Submit. | You | Opens from clean start |

Rules of the clock:

- No new dependencies or refactors after 1:30.
- If the structure is wrong at 1:15, do not polish. Pick another direction (or roll again) and rebuild only the first screen and the main journey.
- Human checkpoint time totals about 15 minutes. If one runs long, accept the AI's recommendation and move.

## 4. Run of show: several short challenges

Per challenge of about 20 to 25 minutes:

| Minutes | What |
|---|---|
| 3 | Pen first: one own idea, one true detail, one must-not |
| 5 | Ten options with probabilities; roll the dice once; three takes |
| 2 | You pick or mix |
| 8 | Produce |
| 4 | Critique against the rubric in `/arena-any`; fix top three |
| 2 | Your own words on the title, first line and last line; save |

If there are more challenges than time, finish fewer, better. A complete strong entry beats two half entries.

## 5. If prompts are judged

- Keep a prompt log as you go (a text file, numbered). Judges reward visible structure.
- A strong prompt has: role and goal; audience; the specific facts (paste your inventory); constraints (length, format, must and must-not); a request for options before output; the output format; anti-examples.
- Show your human input inside the prompt: "Here are my three ideas…", "Here is the detail only I know…".
- Show iteration: "Option B, but keep A's structure and cut X because Y." That is creative direction, and it is the theme of the event.

## 6. If there is an "AI or human?" round

Look for the tells in `.agents/skills/copy-voice/SKILL.md` section 4: the "not just X, it's Y" move, tricolons, uniform sentence rhythm, hedged balance, a closing moral, abstract nouns, many em dashes, words like tapestry, delve, vibrant, testament. Human signs: checkable specifics, uneven rhythm, an opinion that could be wrong, a detail too odd to predict. None of these is proof; weigh several together.

## 7. If you present

Sixty seconds, in this order:

1. The challenge in one line.
2. What you brought: your idea or detail, before any AI.
3. What the AI gave you, and what you rejected ("its first answer was the usual X; I threw it out because…").
4. The idea sentence and the one live thing. Show it working.
5. The human parts: what you chose, cut and wrote yourself.

## 8. Where the AI should not decide

- The idea sentence and the final pick.
- Any fact, name, number, quote or claim.
- What to cut.
- The words in the signature pass.

## 9. Fallbacks

- **AI tool slow or down:** hand-build the chosen spec. Small, coherent and working beats big and broken.
- **Agent cannot see a browser:** you are the screenshot. Look at a narrow and a wide window and describe what you see in plain words.
- **Only a chat tool, no files:** paste docs/ONE-PASTE-PROMPT.md, then drive the same steps by hand. Use the dice table on paper.
- **Image tool only:** use the image prompt pattern in docs/ANY-CHALLENGE-PROMPTS.md; ask for four variations differing in composition, not just colour; pick, then refine one thing at a time.
- **Website builder tool (Lovable, Bolt, v0 and similar):** paste the kick-off with your inventory, ask for the spec first, then the build; paste the anti-slop list as constraints; check the result at phone width.

## 10. Rehearse once

Before the event, run the whole loop once on a practice brief (for example, a page for a local shop or a college fest) with a timer. Note where minutes went and shorten that step. Every checkpoint here is a judgement call, not a proven recipe; trust what your rehearsal shows.
