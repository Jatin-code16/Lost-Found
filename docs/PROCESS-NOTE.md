# Process Note (template and example)

Judges of a "Human Creativity × AI" event want to see where the human was. Keep this short, honest and specific. Fill it in as you go; finish it in the last ten minutes.

## Template

```text
Idea: On this [page / piece], [something only this subject has] becomes [the device].

What I brought before using AI:
- [my three ideas / the true detail / what it must not be]

What the AI did:
- Generated [N] options; I rejected [the rut: …] because […].
- Built [what], from my chosen direction.

What I decided:
- Picked direction [X] (and took [element] from [Y]).
- Cut [thing] because [reason].
- Wrote [headline / first line / button / personal line] myself.

Left out on purpose: [one thing]
Known limit: [one thing]
Checks run: [slop-check tier …; render-check FAIL … WARN …; phone and keyboard checked yes/no]
```

## Example (illustrative practice brief: a page for a kite shop before Makar Sankranti)

```text
Idea: On this page, the shop's thirty-two paper colours become the sky the visitor flies a kite across.

What I brought before using AI:
- My ideas: a kite you can fly with your finger; a price board like the shop's painted one; the founder's 1971 photo.
- True detail: the owner splits bamboo on the floor in the morning; the shop smells of glue.
- Must not be: a generic e-commerce grid, festival clip-art, gold-and-maroon "Indian" cliché.

What the AI did:
- Generated 10 concepts; the two most likely were "festive gradient sky with confetti" and "product grid with a sale banner". I rejected both as the rut.
- Built the page from direction B (told as the shop's hand-painted price board), plus the fly-a-kite interaction from my idea.

What I decided:
- Picked B, kept A's large photo of the founder.
- Cut the countdown timer: it said nothing about this shop.
- Wrote the headline ("Kites cut by hand since 1971") and the button ("Order a dozen") myself.

Left out on purpose: testimonials (we had no real ones).
Known limit: the order button opens an email; there is no checkout.
Checks run: slop-check tier Low; render-check 0 FAIL, 2 WARN (small tap targets on the colour picker, fixed one); checked at phone width and with the keyboard.
```
